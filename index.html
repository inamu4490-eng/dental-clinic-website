Oral-Care Tips

  • Brush twice a day for 2 minutes with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to clean between teeth where brushes can't reach.
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks, which feed cavity-causing bacteria.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3 months.
  • Visit the dentist every 6 months for a check-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people should have a check-up and cleaning every 6 months.

Does teeth whitening damage my teeth?

Professional whitening done by a dentist is safe and does not harm healthy enamel.

At what age should children first see a dentist?

By their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away on (000) 123-4567. For severe pain, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth, come in as soon as possible.
